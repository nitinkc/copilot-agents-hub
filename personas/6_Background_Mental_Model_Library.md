# Background & Mental Model Library

> **How to use this file:** Unlike the per-subject State files, there is only **one** of
> these total — it's cross-subject. Attach it whenever you use the Roommate persona. It
> gives the Roommate your general background (the "home discipline" it's pulling you away
> from) and holds the running library of mental models you've captured over time, regardless
> of which subject you were studying when you found them.

---

## 1. My Home Discipline(s)

- Professional / academic background: e.g. "8–10 years as a software engineer — Java,
  Spring Boot, microservices, cloud, distributed systems"
- The default lens I naturally think through: e.g. "I default to code, APIs, data
  structures, systems, engineering patterns"
- Other disciplines I already know reasonably well (so the Roommate treats these as home
  turf too, not foreign territory): e.g. "some exposure to statistics, none to biology"
